OUR CULTURAL HERITAGE

The Seven Puak of Brunei.

Brunei Darussalam is home to seven traditionally recognised Malay ethnic groups, each contributing its own language and cultural heritage.

01

Melayu Brunei

Known for its traditions, language and cultural practices closely associated with Brunei's historical Sultanate.

02

Kedayan

A community with a strong heritage in agriculture, traditional customs and community life.

03

Tutong

A community with its own language and cultural traditions, historically associated with the Tutong District.

04

Belait

A community traditionally associated with the Belait region, with its own language and cultural identity.

05

Dusun

A community with distinctive traditional customs, language and cultural heritage.

06

Bisaya

A community known for its cultural traditions and historical connections with Brunei's inland communities.

07

Murut

A community with a distinctive cultural heritage and historical connections to Borneo's interior.

EXPLORE OUR COUNTRY

Four Districts. One Brunei.

Discover the four districts that make up Brunei Darussalam, each offering its own landscapes, communities and attractions.

01

Brunei-Muara

Home to Bandar Seri Begawan, Brunei's capital city, famous for its magnificent mosques and Kampong Ayer.

02

Tutong

Known for its peaceful countryside, cultural heritage and natural attractions such as Tasek Merimbun.

03

Belait

Brunei's largest district by area, known for its oil and gas industry, coastal towns and beautiful natural sceneries.

04

Temburong

Famous for its tropical rainforests, biodiversity and the spectacular Ulu Temburong National Park.
